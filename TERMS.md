# Terms

These are draft v0 service terms for the satellite company prototype. Replace them with counsel-reviewed terms before accepting real payment.

## Service

The first service is a compliance proof sprint.

The service may include:

- evidence inventory,
- checklist cleanup,
- monthly proof packet,
- exception notes,
- renewal watchlist,
- exportable receipt.

The service does not provide legal, medical, financial, tax, or regulatory advice.

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- confirming that submitted information is accurate,
- deciding whether the proof packet satisfies their own obligations,
- keeping original records,
- not submitting protected health information, payment credentials, passwords, private keys, or regulated source documents unless a separate reviewed agreement allows it.

## Payment

The v0 route is manual invoice only.

An invoice should not be marked paid until funds actually settle through the real payment route. Refunds, cancellations, failed payments, and tax treatment must follow the reviewed payment and bookkeeping process.

## Delivery

Delivery is complete when the accepted proof packet or agreed deliverable is sent to the customer through the approved support route.

## Limits

The service is provided with reasonable care, but it is not a guarantee of compliance, audit success, revenue, renewal, or legal outcome.

## Separation

Strange Company is a sealed reinvestment prototype. The satellite company is the for-profit operator. Payments to the satellite are not dividends from Strange Company.
