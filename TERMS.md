# Terms

These are draft v0 service terms for the Strange Works Studio satellite operator. They are prepared for a Brazil-first launch path, but they are not legal advice and must be reviewed before accepting real payment.

The customer-facing Portuguese draft is [TERMOS.md](TERMOS.md). If the Brazil-first public desk is used with Brazilian customers, the Portuguese version is the version to review and publish.

## Operator And Jurisdiction

The live operator must be the Brazilian operating entity, CNPJ route, or approved Brazilian operating structure recorded in Setup Evidence.

Do not send paid invoices until the operator has confirmed:

- CNPJ or approved operating structure,
- tax regime and CNAE/accounting path,
- NFS-e or legally reviewed receipt route,
- responsible human operator,
- reviewed customer terms,
- reviewed privacy notice,
- monitored support and incident route.

## Service

The first service is a compliance proof sprint.

The service may include:

- evidence inventory,
- checklist cleanup,
- monthly proof packet,
- exception notes,
- renewal watchlist,
- exportable receipt.

The service does not provide legal, medical, financial, tax, accounting, regulatory, or public-authority advice. AI-created material is draft support for human review and is not a compliance certificate.

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- confirming that submitted information is accurate,
- deciding whether the proof packet satisfies their own obligations,
- keeping original records,
- not submitting protected health information, payment credentials, passwords, private keys, regulated source documents, or sensitive personal data unless a separate reviewed agreement and storage route allow it.

## Data Boundary

The v0 route is for low-risk operational evidence only. The operator may reject, pause, or delete a request that contains regulated documents, secrets, or sensitive personal data outside the reviewed scope.

## Payment, Tax, And Receipts

The v0 route is manual invoice only. The static site never collects card data and never auto-charges customers.

Before paid operation in Brazil, the operator must confirm the payment route and the NFS-e or reviewed fiscal receipt process with an accountant. An invoice should not be marked paid until funds actually settle through the real payment route and the ledger can be reconciled.

Refunds, cancellations, failed payments, taxes, and receipt corrections must follow the reviewed Brazilian payment and bookkeeping process.

## Delivery

Delivery is complete when the accepted proof packet or agreed deliverable is sent to the customer through the approved support route and recorded in the Operations receipt chain.

## Cancellation And Consumer Handling

If a relationship is treated as a consumer relationship under Brazilian law, the operator must handle clear offer information, support access, cancellation/refund routing, and complaint handling under the reviewed consumer-law process before launch.

## Limits

The service is provided with reasonable care, but it is not a guarantee of compliance, audit success, revenue, renewal, legal outcome, tax outcome, or regulator acceptance.

## AI Boundary

AI may draft, organize, summarize, and check for missing evidence. A human operator must approve customer-facing scope, legal/tax statements, refund decisions, privacy responses, incident notices, and any decision that affects a customer's rights or interests.

## Separation

Strange Company is a sealed reinvestment prototype. The satellite company is the revenue-facing operator. Payments to the satellite are not dividends from Strange Company.
