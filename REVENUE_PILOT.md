# Revenue Pilot

Strange Company becomes functional slowly. The first real step is not a full autonomous company. It is one narrow paid pilot with explicit controls.

## Offer

The initial offer is a productized service:

**Compliance proof sprint**

Target customer:

- dental operators,
- clinic administrators,
- small regulated service businesses with scattered compliance evidence.

Promise:

- organize compliance evidence,
- create an exportable proof packet,
- maintain a monthly exception watchlist,
- turn the work into reusable product knowledge.

Default pilot price:

```text
$750/month per pilot customer
```

Target:

```text
$4,000/month committed recurring revenue
```

## Slow Functional Path

1. Record leads.
2. Advance leads through real conversations.
3. Get at least one explicit commitment.
4. Clear the payment blockers.
5. Invoice only after the gate is clear.
6. Deliver manually first.
7. Turn repeated manual work into packets, software, and automation.

## Starting Revenue

The Revenue Pilot now hands off to the Operations `Revenue start` board.

That board keeps the first payment out of the sealed Strange Company lane. The pilot can start revenue only through the second company lane, where Strange Works Studio has separate setup evidence, manual invoice handling, Sheet ledger tracking, support, delivery closeout, and incident handling.

## Payment Gate

The prototype blocks the mental move from interest to revenue until critical blockers are done:

- legal wrapper selected,
- payment route ready,
- accounting lane ready,
- terms and privacy copy ready.

Support route is tracked too, but it is not a critical payment blocker in the prototype.

## Operating Rule

No real money should move through Strange Company until the legal, payment, accounting, and customer terms lanes are real. Before that point, the Revenue Pilot is a commitment tracker and discovery desk.

## Why This Fits The Charter

The pilot does not create employees or dividends. It creates evidence:

- which customer pain is real,
- what people will pay for,
- what work repeats,
- what should become a bounty,
- what should become software,
- what should be killed.

Revenue enters the treasury only after it can be handled legally and cleanly.
