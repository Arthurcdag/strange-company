# Strange Company Game-Theory Rationale

Status: reasoning review from operator discussion on 2026-05-26. This document is not legal, tax, securities, accounting, or investment advice.

## Why This Review Exists

A human reviewer did not understand the Strange Company explanation when it was described as automation, game theory, VAU, and internal value growth. That is useful evidence.

The explanation was too abstract and could be confused with:

- an investment scheme,
- a trading service,
- a promise of monthly yield,
- an automated fund manager,
- or a token/currency value-support project.

The refined rationale must keep the useful core while rejecting those dangerous interpretations.

## Clean Thesis

Strange Company is a sealed compounding operator.

It should:

1. earn revenue from lawful products or services,
2. keep surplus inside the company after taxes, obligations, reserves, and operating costs,
3. allocate that surplus through a gated treasury process,
4. test many small actions,
5. kill weak branches,
6. scale branches that survive evidence review,
7. and avoid dependency on one CEO, one trader, one employee, one platform, or one narrative.

The game-theory point is not "beat the market." The point is to create a company whose incentives make reinvestment, auditability, and survival more stable than personal extraction.

## Two-Company Separation

The conversation makes the two-company model clearer:

| Lane | Role | Allowed purpose | Forbidden confusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strange Company | Sealed compounding engine | Own-company revenue, treasury gates, reinvestment, automation, durable assets | Customer-money management, dividends, guaranteed returns, speculative public promises |
| Satellite company | Normal operator | Sell services, earn ordinary profit, pay taxes, support customers, fund the founder/operator | Pretend related-party revenue proves market demand, move sealed treasury value to insiders without real service |

Strange Company is not the normal income vehicle. The satellite can be normal. That distinction should be repeated whenever the model is explained.

## VAU And Game Theory In Plain Language

VAU does not mean the future is known.

VAU means:

- generate many possible futures,
- identify actions that are useful across many futures,
- block actions that require outside proof,
- run small real probes,
- observe what happened,
- update the model,
- and reject branches that reality disproves.

The "game theory" layer means the company assumes other agents react. Customers, competitors, regulators, vendors, attackers, and operators can all change behavior after seeing the system. Therefore, Strange Company should prefer strategies that remain defensible when other players adapt.

Useful game-theory properties:

- no single human can capture the treasury,
- no single vendor can hold the company hostage,
- no one gets paid merely because a narrative sounds profitable,
- spending proposals must survive evidence review,
- failed experiments cool down instead of receiving more capital,
- and good outcomes become new claims, not automatic approvals.

## Currency Or Value Language

The phrase "valorizar a moeda" is unsafe if it suggests a public token, investment contract, market manipulation, or promise that outside holders will profit.

Safer wording:

> Strange Company tries to increase its own durable enterprise value by reinvesting retained surplus into useful assets, products, infrastructure, distribution, security, and compliance.

Forbidden wording:

- "guaranteed return"
- "minimum monthly return"
- "we manage your money"
- "we make the token go up"
- "safe daily percentage"
- "clients deposit capital and we trade it"
- "AI chooses trades for customers"

If a token, coin, fund, managed account, or customer-capital product is ever considered, it must move to a separate legal/regulatory review track before any public claim, customer intake, custody, trading, or marketing exists.

## Investment And Trading Firewall

Strange Company must not become a trading desk for other people.

Hard rules:

- Do not accept customer capital for trading.
- Do not pool customer funds.
- Do not promise fixed, minimum, daily, or monthly returns.
- Do not let VAU, Codex, AI, or Research Gate approve trades.
- Do not use customer funds, payment balances, tax reserves, refund reserves, or payroll/vendor funds for speculation.
- Do not describe internal treasury allocation as investment management.
- Do not count external trader screenshots, claimed daily percentage, or claimed customer balances as Strange Company evidence.

Allowed research-only use:

- record a counterparty claim as an external signal,
- ask what would need verification,
- route it to human/legal/compliance review if relevant,
- or reject it as outside scope.

## Brazil Regulatory Boundary

In Brazil, managing investment portfolios for others is a regulated activity. Official CVM guidance says professional management of securities portfolios requires prior CVM authorization. The investor guidance also says portfolio administrators must be registered with CVM and meet regulatory criteria.

Crypto and virtual-asset services also sit inside a regulatory perimeter. Banco Central guidance says it regulates, authorizes, and supervises virtual-asset service providers, and BCB Resolution 520 disciplines the operation of virtual-asset service providers.

Practical consequence for Strange Company:

> Until qualified counsel and the responsible regulator say otherwise in writing, Strange Company should treat customer-money trading, managed accounts, pooled capital, guaranteed yield, crypto custody, exchange/intermediation, and offshore yield structures as blocked.

Reference:

- CVM administrator registration service: `https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/registrar-administrador-de-carteira-cvm`
- CVM investor page on portfolio managers: `https://www.gov.br/investidor/pt-br/investir/como-investir/profissionais-do-mercado/gestor-administrador-de-carteira-de-valores-mobiliarios`
- CVM investor-protection bulletins: `https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/protecao/guias-e-boletins`
- BCB virtual-asset service FAQ: `https://www.bcb.gov.br/meubc/faqs/p/guarda-e-negociacao-de-moedas-virtuais`
- BCB Resolution 520/2025: `https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/exibenormativo?numero=520&tipo=Resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o+BCB`

## Human Scrutiny Request

The useful human-review ask is not:

> Please believe the AI system.

The useful ask is:

> Please attack the assumptions, find where this becomes illegal, fragile, misleading, or impossible, and help define the evidence needed before any real money, customer promise, or public claim is allowed.

Minimum reviewer questions:

1. Is this claim actually about company revenue, or is it secretly about taking investor money?
2. Does any wording imply guaranteed return or customer-money management?
3. Is the action funded by Strange Company's own retained surplus only?
4. Is there a real product/customer/service behind the revenue?
5. Does this require CVM, Banco Central, tax, LGPD, consumer-law, or accounting review?
6. Could a buyer misunderstand this as an investment offer?
7. Can the action be reversed, capped, and audited?
8. What evidence would falsify the claim?

A separate satellite company can operate as a normal service business.

## Evolved Decision Rule

Use this rule when a finance-like idea enters the system:

```text
If the action uses company-owned surplus to improve a lawful product, asset, operation, or resilience layer, send it to Research Gate.

If the action uses customer/investor money, promises yield, manages portfolios, touches crypto custody/intermediation, or depends on regulatory arbitrage, block it and route to professional review.
```

This preserves the core Strange Company idea while preventing the rationale from drifting into an investment product.
