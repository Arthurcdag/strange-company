## One-Minute Summary

Strange Company is meant to be a sealed compounding company:

- no CEO with unilateral control,
- no dividends by default,
- no permanent employee dependency,
- revenue from real products or services,
- surplus retained inside the company,
- treasury spend only after evidence review,
- small experiments before larger allocation,
- and human review where law, tax, privacy, payment, or customer claims require it.

It is not meant to:

- take customer capital for trading,
- manage investor money,
- promise monthly or daily returns,
- custody crypto for customers,
- operate a fund,
- manipulate a token or public currency,
- or use AI as a replacement for legal/accounting/human review.

## Review Questions

Please attack the model with these questions:

1. Does any wording sound like an investment offer?
2. Does any wording imply guaranteed return, minimum return, or safe yield?
3. Does the model clearly separate company-owned surplus from customer/investor money?
4. Does the satellite company separation reduce conflict, or does it create a related-party risk?
5. Does VAU sound like a decision-support tool, or like a fake proof engine?
6. Are the live-operation blockers clear enough?
7. What would need lawyer, accountant, CVM, Banco Central, tax, LGPD, payment-provider, or bank review before launch?
8. What claim should be removed, weakened, or rewritten before this is shown publicly?

## Stop Rules

If any answer below is "yes", the reviewed action should stay blocked:

- Does it use customer or investor money?

- Does it depend on trading performance?
- Does it custody, intermediate, or exchange crypto assets for third parties?
- Does it ask AI to approve legal, tax, privacy, payment, or investment decisions?
- Could a normal person misunderstand it as a financial product?
