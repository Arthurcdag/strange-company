# Support

The v0 support route is manual and must be monitored before live customers are accepted.

## Inbox

Target inbox:

```text
ops@strangeworks.studio
```

This inbox must exist and be monitored before the Operations console is marked ready.

## Response Targets

- New customer order: one business day.
- Active delivery question: one business day.
- Payment or cancellation issue: one business day.
- Security, privacy, or incident report: same day triage.

## Incident Path

If a customer reports a security, privacy, payment, or delivery incident:

1. acknowledge receipt,
2. stop related delivery work,
3. preserve the order and invoice packet,
4. record the issue in the decision log,
5. escalate to legal, accounting, or security support if needed,
6. publish a corrective receipt after resolution.

## Support Boundaries

The first service does not accept protected health information, payment credentials, passwords, private keys, or regulated source documents.

If a customer needs regulated handling, pause the order until a reviewed agreement, storage route, and support process exist.
